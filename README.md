# LevelUp — Solo Leveling System HUD

A real-life gamification HUD inspired by the "System" interface from *Solo Leveling*. Features include **AI Vision Auto-Quest Detection (MediaPipe Pose Tracking)**, **Live AR Camera Mode (Meta AI Glasses Testing)**, **Startup Hunter Registration**, **Interactive Quest Creation Form (`[ + ADD QUEST ]`)**, **Persistent Storage (`data/quests.json` / `localStorage`)**, **Dual Viewports** (**Desktop 1024×768** and **Mobile 450×850**), **Touch/Mouse Taps**, **Voice Commands**, an **On-Screen Text Console**, and **Keyboard Shortcuts**.

---

## 🤖 AI Vision Auto-Quest Detection (MediaPipe Pose)

The System can now automatically detect real-world physical exercises (Pushups) through your camera and complete quests on its own without touching any buttons!

1. Open your live website:
   👉 **[https://zareen333.github.io/levelup-system-hud/](https://zareen333.github.io/levelup-system-hud/)**
2. Tap the **`[ 🤖 AI AUTO-DETECT ]`** button in the header bar.
3. Perform pushups or arm bicep flexion in front of your phone or laptop camera.
4. The live AI Rep Counter will track your reps: `[ AI REPS: 1/5 ]` → `[ AI REPS: 5/5 ]`.
5. Once target reps are hit, the **AI automatically marks the quest completed on its own**, grants +50 XP, and speaks TTS voice verification!

---

## 👓 Meta AI Glasses Live AR Camera Mode

Test the HUD over the real world through your smartphone or laptop camera:

1. Tap **`[ 👓 AR CAMERA ]`** in the header bar.
2. Your phone's camera stream will display live behind the translucent glowing cyan/violet HUD panels with Sci-Fi AR scanlines!
3. Tap **`[ 🔄 FLIP CAM ]`** to switch between rear environment camera and front selfie camera.
